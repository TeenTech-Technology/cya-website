What we collect and why

We collect personal information that you choose to send us, plus a simple count of page visits. We do not have a donations page. This website does not take payments, and we do not ask for bank or mobile-money details.

Contact forms (Home page and Contact page)

We collect your name, email address, the topic you choose, and your message. We use this to read your message and reply. The form is sent through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to infocya260@gmail.com. The email includes the fact that you ticked the consent box.

Event seat reservations (Register page and Events page)

We collect your full name, phone number, email address if you give one, the event, and how many seats you ask for. We use this to reserve your seat, show how many seats are left, and check tickets at the door. This is stored in Supabase, the database CYA uses for events and seats. Your ticket appears on your screen. Please save it. CYA does not send the ticket by email or WhatsApp.

News list

The “Stay in the loop” box in the footer, and “Keep me posted” on the Register page when no event is open, collect your email address and your name if you type one. We use this to send occasional news about CYA events and programmes. This is stored in Supabase.

Volunteer form

We collect your full name, phone number, email address if you give one, your area, your age range if you choose one (the list includes “Under 18”), your skills and interests, when you are free, a message if you write one, and the fact that you agreed we may contact you. We use this so the CYA team can talk to you about volunteering. This is stored in Supabase, including your consent tick.

Partnership enquiry

We collect the organisation’s name, the contact person’s name, a phone number, an email address, the type of partnership, and a message if you write one. We use this to reply about working with CYA. This is stored in Supabase.

Event feedback

We collect the event you choose, your rating from 1 to 5, comments if you write any, and your name if you give it. We use this to improve future events. This is stored in Supabase. You can leave your name blank.

Visit counts

We store the address of the page you open and, if you arrived from another website, the name of that website. We use this to see which pages people use. It is stored in Supabase. Each page is counted once per browser tab. The website uses session storage on your device for that (it is cleared when you close the tab). The website’s own code does not store your IP address for this count.

How it is stored and who we share it with

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub serves the pages you read. CYA does not control GitHub’s systems.
  • FormSubmit delivers the Home and Contact forms to the inbox infocya260@gmail.com. FormSubmit is a separate service. CYA does not control it.
  • Supabase stores events, seat reservations, the news list, volunteer forms, partnership enquiries, event feedback and visit counts. The CYA team uses those records to run events and reply to people.
  • The website talks to FormSubmit and Supabase over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

We do not sell personal information. We do not pass it to advertisers.

GitHub, FormSubmit, Supabase or the email service may process information outside Zambia. Under the Data Protection Act, sending personal data out of Zambia needs a lawful basis.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 you can ask CYA to:

  • Access your information: tell you what we hold about you
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • Delete your information, where the law allows it

You can also ask us to stop sending news emails. A parent or guardian may ask on behalf of a child.

To ask, email infocya260@gmail.com, call 0965175304 (+260965175304), or visit Room One, Town Center, Chingola. Tell us your name, how we can reach you, and what you want (for example “delete my seat reservation” or “correct my phone number”).

Children and parental consent

CYA is a youth group. Many people who use this site are under 18. The Data Protection Act gives extra protection to children. If you are under 18, please get a parent or guardian to agree before you send your name, phone number, email or any other personal information.

Please do not send someone else’s personal information unless you are allowed to. The volunteer form lets you choose the age range “Under 18”. The seat form does not ask your age.

A parent or guardian can contact us to see, correct or delete a child’s information.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use Google Analytics or other third-party analytics tools. CYA has not added cookies to these pages. The code that runs the site does not set a cookie.

The only measurement is the visit count described above. It uses session storage in your browser, not a cookie. Session storage stays on your device until you close the tab.
