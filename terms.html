Events and seat reservations

Reserving a seat is how you register for an event. You give your name and phone number, and an email address if you want to. You choose how many seats, up to the number the form allows.

After you reserve, a ticket with a code and a QR code appears on your screen. Please download it, print it, or take a screenshot, and show it at the entrance. CYA does not send tickets by email or WhatsApp. If you do not save the ticket, CYA cannot send you a copy from this website.

Seats can run out. A date may still say that it is to be confirmed. CYA may change the date, time, venue or programme of an event, or cancel it.

The Share button lets you send a link to an event by WhatsApp, Facebook, email, or your phone’s share menu. You choose what to send. CYA does not receive your contact list.
