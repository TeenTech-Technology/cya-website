About Us

The central hub for youth empowerment in Chingola

Chingola Youth Association (CYA) is a vibrant, youth-led non-profit organization registered under the National Youth Development Council (NYDC). Founded as the central hub for youth empowerment and developmental initiatives in Chingola, Zambia, CYA is dedicated to building a generation of skilled, responsible, and engaged young citizens.


More about CYA

Our Mission

To empower the youth of Chingola through skills development, leadership training, entrepreneurship, health advocacy, Climate Change and inclusive partnerships.

Our Vision

A transformed Chingola where young people are at the forefront of innovation, leadership, and sustainable development.

Core Values

Integrity Innovation Collaboration Excellence Inclusiveness Resilience

Chingola Youth Indaba 2025

Coordinated by CYA and declared an annual event, attracting over 200 youths from all walks of life. Graced by His Worship Johnson Kang’ombe, Mayor of Chingola.

Aquaculture project with KCM & Green Initiative

Promoting sustainable aquaculture and mining in Zambia, improving climate justice.

Mentorship sessions

For different schools on the Copperbelt on public speaking, leadership training, critical thinking, climate justice and governance.

Menstrual health & sanitary pads

With Let’s Inspire Youth Association (LIYA), educated young girls at Kachema Musuma Community School and Chiwempala Combined Secondary School, donating over 300 sanitary pads.
